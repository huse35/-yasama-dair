My story

Words, memories and the meaning of sharing, drawn from a life lived.

What we share is what remains.

These words once filled notebooks. Now they have a place here. The greatest lesson from 64 years: life grows richer when we share it.

Here you will find words, the quiet of lived experience and the honest stories that follow. The melancholy in a photograph, the joy in a recording: each holds a little piece of life.

I am here to share a word, a memory or a question that stays with me. Sometimes we will pause in front of a photograph. Sometimes we will follow a thought.

Life is not what we accumulate, but what we share and who we share it with.
